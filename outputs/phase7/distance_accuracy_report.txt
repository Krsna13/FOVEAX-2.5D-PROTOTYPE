Summary & Engineering Findings:
  1. SemanticKITTI (HDL-64E urban domain): Achieves ~93.3% overall agreement.
     High accuracy across Near (94.7%) and Mid (92.5%) zones.
  2. RELLIS-3D Unadapted (HDL-64E assumption on OS1-64): Suffers catastrophic failure (~11.1%).
     Sensor FOV mismatch (+17.02/-16.44 vs +3/-25) causes 85%+ false vehicle hallucinations.
  3. RELLIS-3D Sensor-Adapted (Correct FOV + intensity rescale): Triples agreement to ~37.1%.
     Vegetation agreement jumps to >75%, eliminating the projection collapse.
  4. Off-Road Limitation: While geometric sensor adaptation fixes physical projection,
     unadapted urban backbones still confuse dirt trails with asphalt/vehicles.
     Fine-tuning SalsaNext on RELLIS-3D labels is required for full trail autonomy.
